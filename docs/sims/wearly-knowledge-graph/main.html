What size means

Larger nodes carry more user signal — a heavier WardrobeItem is worn more often or covers more occasions; a heavier ColorFamily shows up across more of your closet; a heavier OccasionType has richer wardrobe coverage. Domain archetypes (RulePack, WeatherCondition) stay at base size — they're equal-importance facts, not user-weighted.
